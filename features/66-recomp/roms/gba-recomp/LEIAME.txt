=== English ===
Put your recompiled games here, with the console turned off. Each game is a script (.sh) in this folder
that starts the game; the game's files (its binary, whatever its extension, and the saves) go in a folder next to it.
Turn the console on: the .sh files show up in the "GBA Recomp" system.
To leave a game: hold FN and press Start.
